问题The gap

奖励承诺很容易。
支付证明却很少。Reward promises are easy.
Payment proof is rare.

数字劳动已经发生在 GitHub、Agent 和社区任务中，但支付通常停留在私下转账、口头承诺或零散截图里。Digital labor already happens across GitHub, Agents, and community tasks, but payment often remains a private transfer, a promise, or a scattered screenshot.

真正愿意付费的项目，应该能够证明自己确实付过钱。Projects that genuinely pay should be able to prove that they paid.从奖励宣传到支付信用From reward marketing to payment credibility
01外部参与者看不到项目是否兑现过奖励。Outsiders cannot see whether a project has ever fulfilled its rewards.
02项目方难以把贡献、规则和实际支付关联起来。Projects struggle to connect contribution, policy, and the actual payment.
03人工流程无法稳定处理归属、重试、重复支付和事后审计。Manual flows do not reliably handle attribution, retries, duplicates, and post-payment audit.

让数字贡献拥有

每一份真实贡献，都可以被识别、被奖励、被验证，并成为项目 支付信用 的一部分。Every real contribution can be recognized, rewarded, verified, and become part of a project's payment credibility.

贡献证据Contribution evidence

02

支付规则Payment policy

把奖励对象、金额、链和地址绑定到确定性策略。Bind recipient, amount, chain, and address to deterministic policy.

03

公开证明Public proof

将已验证的结算结果发布出来，让项目积累可复核的支付记录。Publish verified settlement results so projects build a reviewable payment record.

工作流How it works

不是自动转账。
是公开结算。Not an automated transfer.
A public settlement.

ReleaseRail 把 Agent 的发现能力和确定性资金执行隔离开来，让每一步都能被人确认、被系统约束、被链上验证。ReleaseRail separates an Agent's discovery from deterministic fund execution, so every step can be reviewed by people, constrained by code, and verified onchain.

01 / EVIDENCE

验证贡献Verify contribution

确认 commit 真的进入指定 Release。Confirm the commit actually shipped in the named release.

02 / POLICY

生成意图Create intent

锁定收款人、金额、资产、链和规则版本。Lock recipient, amount, asset, chain, and policy version.

03 / APPROVAL

人工确认Approve

确认完整 canonical hash 后，才允许继续。Require approval of the exact canonical hash.

04 / EXECUTION

受控支付Execute safely

通过 KeeperHub 模拟、执行并处理幂等重试。Simulate, execute, and handle retries through KeeperHub.

05 / PROOF

公开证明Publish proof

验证 receipt，并留下公开交易记录。Verify the receipt and leave a public transaction record.
